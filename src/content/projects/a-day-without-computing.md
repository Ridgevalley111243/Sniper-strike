## The idea
For Ridgevalley Hybrid School's anniversary, I built a presentation around one question: what would a day without computing look like?

## The story
- School life and the school campus.
- Learners in the school's green uniform.
- Parents paying fees with their phones.
- Before-and-after scenes, with and without computing.
- Characters and narration explaining why technology matters, including AI-generated characters making announcements and inviting people to the event.

## The craft
The project combined storytelling, visual design and educational communication, using AI tools to speed up character and scene production.
