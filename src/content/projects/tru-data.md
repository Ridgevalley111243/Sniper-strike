## The idea
Buying data bundles should be quick, safe and available any time. TRU DATA is my digital commerce venture: a storefront where customers sign in, choose a network and bundle, pay online and receive their data automatically.

## What it brings together
- **Storefront:** bundle packages and network options for MTN, AirtelTigo and Telecel.
- **Accounts:** customers log in before purchasing.
- **Payments:** Paystack integration, with payment issues investigated and resolved.
- **Automated delivery:** integration with the Refer2Bundle API for balances, available bundles, single and bulk orders, and order status.
- **Administration:** managing prices, orders, sales and day-to-day operations.
- **Agents:** agent accounts with commissions and individual prices, protected by rules that keep pricing profitable.
- **Withdrawals:** requests with limits and administrator approval.
- **Support & revenue:** WhatsApp customer support and AdSense advertising.

## Beyond the web
I am also packaging TRU DATA as an Android app (`com.trudata.app`): configuring SDK versions and app icons, exploring AdMob banner and rewarded ads, and testing on Samsung Galaxy devices.

## Where it stands
The website is online and the custom domain trudata.shop is planned. Some integrations are still being completed.
