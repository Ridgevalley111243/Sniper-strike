## The need
Running a school on paper and spreadsheets makes records hard to find, results slow to compile and fee balances difficult to track. Ridgevalley Hybrid School needed one system for administrators, the headmaster, teachers and parents.

## What the system covers
- **Students:** registration, records, photographs, class assignments and bulk imports from Excel.
- **Academics:** subjects, academic years, terms, assessments, examination results and student reports.
- **Attendance:** recording and monitoring daily attendance.
- **School fees:** payments, outstanding balances and fee records, with Paystack online payments alongside cash and mobile money.
- **Role-based access:** separate access for the super administrator, school administrator, headmaster, teachers and parents.
- **Reporting:** results, student records and administrative reports.
- **Branding:** dark mode, school branding, animations and the school's green-and-red colour scheme.

## Solving real problems
Building a system people depend on means getting the data right. Along the way I diagnosed and fixed issues such as mismatched class identifiers, teachers not being found by database queries, and student records not appearing correctly in reports. Each fix meant understanding the data structure, testing the solution and checking the behaviour for every type of user.

## Where it stands
The system is in active development, built to become a practical administrative tool for the school, not just a demonstration website.
