## The brief
Trutech Business needed an identity that feels professional and trustworthy for corporate clients, yet friendly and approachable for schools and individuals.

## The identity
- **Mark:** three leaves growing from one point: ideas growing into something real.
- **Wordmark:** TRUTECH in a strong geometric sans, with BUSINESS spaced beneath in light blue.
- **Palette:** #0A1931, #1A3D63, #4A7FA7, #B3CFE5 and #F6FAFD, from deep navy to soft mist.
- **Assets:** transparent logo files for light and dark backgrounds, icons and social images.

This website uses the same identity throughout.
