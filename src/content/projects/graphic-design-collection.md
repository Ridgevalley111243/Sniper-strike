## The work
Print and social media designs made for real clients and for Trutech Business:

- **Restaurant menus:** Jollof Mistress and Hajia Samsia Fast Foods, with food photography, clear prices and bold brand colours.
- **School events:** the Kumasi Academy inter-department debate and Ridgevalley Hybrid School's X'mas Before Christmas, with every detail parents and students need at a glance.
- **Seasonal campaigns:** new month, December and Valentine's Day posts that keep Trutech Business and TRU DATA in front of customers, with QR codes and social handles built in.

## How I work
I design in Canva, Affinity, Photopea and PixelLab, with attention to consistent colours, sharp output, clean transparent backgrounds and natural-looking photo edits. Each design starts from what the reader must take away (the menu prices, the event date, the offer) and builds the visuals around it.
